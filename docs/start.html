    2. Copy the screening prompt: the criteria C1–C6 are already pasted in.
    3. Paste both into a new temporary chat, web search off: the prompt, then the records where it says [PASTE THE RECORDS HERE].
    4. Paste the AI's answer into the scorer, as it is: no file to save.
    Open the records Open the scorer
    Show the screening prompt 
    You are a second reviewer screening titles and abstracts for a systematic review.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA (apply exactly these, nothing else):
C1 Setting. Include: Acute care hospital (general, tertiary referral or teaching hospital providing short-term inpatient care). Exclude: Only long-term care, nursing homes, outpatient or ambulatory care, rehabilitation; veterinary settings.
C2 Scope. Include: Implemented hospital-wide or across more than one inpatient ward or department. Exclude: Only a single ICU, neonatal ICU, emergency department or any other isolated single unit.
C3 Intervention. Include: Multimodal hand hygiene intervention (at least two components, e.g. education, reminders, audit and feedback, hand rub availability, leadership, champions) that is the clearly central focus. Exclude: A single isolated measure (posters alone, glove policy, product comparison, electronic monitoring alone); broad infection-prevention bundles (screening, isolation, cohorting, cleaning, stewardship) where hand hygiene is one component whose contribution cannot be interpreted.
C4 Design. Include: Interrupted time series, controlled before-after, or uncontrolled before-after with clearly defined pre- and post-intervention periods. Exclude: Randomised trials (including cluster), cross-sectional or descriptive studies, outbreak reports, case reports, editorials, letters, conference abstracts, reviews.
C5 Outcome. Include: At least one clinical HAI outcome (overall HAI rate, CLABSI, CAUTI, VAP, C. difficile infection, MRSA or VRE acquisition or infection, other defined hospital-acquired infection). Exclude: Only process measures (compliance, hand rub consumption, knowledge, microbial counts on hands).
C6 Language. Include: English (no date limits). Exclude: Other languages.

RULES
- Decide for each record: include, exclude, or unsure.
- exclude ONLY if the title/abstract clearly shows that at least one criterion is NOT met. Name the criterion.
- include if all criteria are met or not contradicted by the abstract.
- unsure if information is missing or ambiguous. When in doubt, do not exclude.
- Use only the text given. Do not search the web, and do not use anything you may know about these studies.
- Return one row for EVERY record, in the same order. If the abstract is empty, decide on the title only and use unsure with reason "no abstract".

OUTPUT: only a CSV block with the header
record_id,decision,reason
where reason is max 25 words and starts with the criterion code (e.g. "C2 not met: ...").
After the CSV, write: "Records received: N. Rows returned: N."

RECORDS
[PASTE THE RECORDS HERE]

Reminder: apply only the criteria above; one row per record; when in doubt, unsure.

    Open the paper Extraction answers 
    
            
    
              Show the extraction prompt
              
    You are extracting data for a systematic review. Use ONLY the paper I give you. Do not search the web or use outside knowledge.

EXTRACTION FORM (fill every field, do not add or change fields):
- country: Country where the study was conducted. Take it from Methods/Results; if the paper gives it only in the affiliation of the study hospital, report it with 'author affiliations' as location. Do not infer it from city or campaign names alone; if it is not stated anywhere, write not reported. Allowed values: free text.
- study_design: Design of the evaluation, as you would classify it (not only the authors' label). Allowed values: interrupted time series | controlled before-after | uncontrolled before-after | randomised trial | other.
- intervention_components: Components of the hand hygiene intervention. Allowed values: list, separated by ';': education/training; alcohol-based hand rub at point of care; reminders/posters; audit and feedback; leadership/institutional safety climate; patient participation; other (specify).
- primary_hai_outcome: The main clinical healthcare-associated infection outcome reported (name it as the authors do). Allowed values: free text.
- direction_of_effect: Direction of the effect on the primary HAI outcome. Allowed values: favourable | no clear effect | mixed | unfavourable.

RULES
- For every field give: value, a verbatim quote copied exactly from the paper, and the location (section, table, page).
- If the paper does not report the field, write: not reported. Do not guess.
- Do not round or convert. If there are several values (several wards, arms or time periods), list each on its own row and say which is which.

OUTPUT: only a CSV block with the header
study_id,field,value,source_quote,location
Use study_id = Phan2020.

PAPER:
[PASTE THE TEXT OF THE PAPER, OR SAY "attached"]

4Working on your own

      
No groups, no roles: you drive the AI, then you verify it. First let the AI answer; then stop and check what it said against the abstract or the paper before you believe it. Comparing your errors with a neighbour after step 2 is optional. A laptop is recommended; a phone works for reading the pages. More

    


    
    

      
5Three rules

      

        

          
          
Turn off web search / browsing and use a temporary chat (memory off).
The reference review is published: an AI with web access may simply look up the answers. At a previous workshop in Berlin, one recognised the review and offered to repeat it.

        

        

          
          
Screening answers after step 1, extraction answers after step 3.
Not earlier: not the answers page, not the reference review, not the facilitator guide. They name the included studies.

        

        

          
          
For learning only. You stay responsible for every decision: the AI does not.
Paste only texts whose licence allows it, and never personal or confidential data.

        

      

    


    
    

      

        

          
Not in the room? Do it on your own

          
Everything is online. Allow 60 to 90 minutes; no facilitator or slides needed.
