    Show the prompt 
    You are a second reviewer screening titles and abstracts for a systematic review.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA (apply exactly these, nothing else):
C1 Setting. Include: Acute care hospital (general, tertiary referral or teaching hospital providing short-term inpatient care). Exclude: Only long-term care, nursing homes, outpatient or ambulatory care, rehabilitation; veterinary settings.
C2 Scope. Include: Implemented hospital-wide or across more than one inpatient ward or department. Exclude: Only a single ICU, neonatal ICU, emergency department or any other isolated single unit.
C3 Intervention. Include: Multimodal hand hygiene intervention (at least two components, e.g. education, reminders, audit and feedback, hand rub availability, leadership, champions) that is the clearly central focus. Exclude: A single isolated measure (posters alone, glove policy, product comparison, electronic monitoring alone); broad infection-prevention bundles (screening, isolation, cohorting, cleaning, stewardship) where hand hygiene is one component whose contribution cannot be interpreted.
C4 Design. Include: Interrupted time series, controlled before-after, or uncontrolled before-after with clearly defined pre- and post-intervention periods. Exclude: Randomised trials (including cluster), cross-sectional or descriptive studies, outbreak reports, case reports, editorials, letters, conference abstracts, reviews.
C5 Outcome. Include: At least one clinical HAI outcome (overall HAI rate, CLABSI, CAUTI, VAP, C. difficile infection, MRSA or VRE acquisition or infection, other defined hospital-acquired infection). Exclude: Only process measures (compliance, hand rub consumption, knowledge, microbial counts on hands).
C6 Language. Include: English (no date limits). Exclude: Other languages.

RULES
- Decide for each record: include, exclude, or unsure.
- exclude ONLY if the title/abstract clearly shows that at least one criterion is NOT met. Name the criterion.
- include if all criteria are met or not contradicted by the abstract.
- unsure if information is missing or ambiguous. When in doubt, do not exclude.
- Use only the text given. Do not use anything you may know about these studies.
- Return one row for EVERY record, in the same order. If the abstract is empty, decide on the title only and use unsure with reason "no abstract".

OUTPUT: only a CSV block with the header
record_id,decision,reason
where reason is max 25 words and starts with the criterion code (e.g. "C2 not met: ...").
After the CSV, write: "Records received: N. Rows returned: N."

RECORDS
[PASTE THE RECORDS HERE]

Reminder: apply only the criteria above; one row per record; when in doubt, unsure.

4Your group roles

3 to 4 people, one screen. Agree in the first minute; rotate if you like. More on the roles

  • Drivertypes, uploads, runs the tool; one screen for the whole group
  • Prompterwrites the prompts; reads the criteria to the AI, not from memory
  • Verifierchecks the AI against the source: every quote and number
  • Recorderfills the AI use record and the discrepancy log

5Two rules

Do not open the answers before step 3.

Not the answers/ folder, not the reference review, not the facilitator guide: they name the included studies.

For learning only. You stay responsible for every decision: the AI does not.

Paste only texts whose licence allows it, and never personal or confidential data.

Not in the room? Do it on your own

Everything is online. Allow 60 to 90 minutes; no facilitator, slides or group needed.
