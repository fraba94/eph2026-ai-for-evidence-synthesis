Chat track · records

Records for your chat

Pick a batch, press Copy batch, and paste it into your chat assistant right after the screening prompt. Missing abstracts are fetched from PubMed directly in your browser.

Sample mode. These are real PubMed records found with the search "hand hygiene healthcare-associated infection", only to show how the page works. They are not the workshop records. Switch to the workshop records.

For learning only. These prompts are teaching material for this workshop. They are not a validated method. If you are not confident with how large language models work (context windows, parsing, sampling, retrieval, hallucination) and with advanced techniques (structured outputs, verification against the source, reproducibility checks, documentation of model and version), do not use AI to conduct a real evidence review. You stay responsible for every decision: the AI does not.

Records

Loading the workshop records...

Use another records file

Any CSV with the columns record_id,pmid,title,abstract (the workshop format is record_id,pmid,doi,title,abstract,year,journal,license).

2

Pick a batch and copy it

Smaller batches are safer: long messages are where assistants start skipping records.

3

Then

  • Use the screening prompt from the beginners guide or the prompt library, then paste the batch.
  • Count the rows you get back: every record must appear exactly once. If the answer is shorter, ask for the missing IDs.
  • Records marked [no abstract available]: decide on the title or mark them unsure. Never let the assistant write the abstract from memory.
  • Save the answer as record_id,decision,reason and score it in the scorer.

Abstracts not included in the workshop file are requested from PubMed (NCBI E-utilities) by your browser, at most 20 PMIDs per request, and are not stored. Abstract text remains under the copyright of its publishers; use it only for this exercise.
